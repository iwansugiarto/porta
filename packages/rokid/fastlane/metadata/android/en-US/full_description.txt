Porta is a mobile bridge app that connects your phone to AI coding assistants. Monitor and interact with your AI pair programming sessions in real-time from anywhere.

Key Features:
• Real-time streaming of AI coding conversations
• Approve or reject AI-proposed commands directly from your phone
• View code diffs, file changes, and terminal output on the go
• Voice input for hands-free interaction
• Secure WebSocket connection with TLS/SSL support
• Persistent settings — configure once, connect automatically
• Dark mode optimized interface

Porta bridges the gap between your development workstation and your mobile device, giving you full visibility and control over your AI coding workflows.
